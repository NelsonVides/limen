Welcome to the archive of our regional programme, where you can find every report we have published since the first season.
The archive is organised by year and by topic, and each entry links to the documents that were shared at the time.
Most of the material in the archive was prepared by volunteers who wanted to keep a record of the work in the region.
If you are looking for a specific report, the index at the bottom of this page lists every title in order.
We update the index at the end of each month, so recent additions may take a few weeks to appear.
Some of the older documents were scanned from paper copies and may be difficult to read on small screens.
You can request a printed copy of any report by writing to the office with the title and the year of publication.

Our team works with partners across the region to improve the way local services are planned and delivered.
The team meets every week to review the progress of each project and to agree on the next steps.
Each project has a coordinator who is responsible for the schedule, the budget and the communication with partners.
We publish a short summary of every meeting so that members can follow the discussion from anywhere.
The summaries are written in plain language and avoid technical terms whenever possible.
If a term needs to be explained, we add a short note at the end of the summary.
Members are welcome to suggest topics for the next meeting through the form on the community page.

This guide explains how to prepare an application for the next round of the programme.
Before you begin, read the eligibility criteria carefully and check that your project meets each of them.
The application form asks for a description of the project, a timeline and an estimate of the costs.
You can save the form at any time and return to it later, as long as you submit it before the deadline.
Applications that arrive after the deadline cannot be considered, even if the delay was caused by a technical problem.
We recommend that you prepare the description in a separate document and copy it into the form when it is ready.
A member of the team will confirm that your application was received within two working days.
If you have not received a confirmation after a week, please contact the office.
The review of applications usually takes six to eight weeks, depending on the number of submissions.
During the review, we may ask for additional information or clarification about your budget.
Successful applicants will be invited to a short meeting to discuss the details of the agreement.

Every spring we organise an open day at the main office, where visitors can meet the team and learn about our work.
The open day includes short presentations, a guided tour of the building and a small exhibition of recent projects.
Entry is free, but we ask visitors to register in advance so that we can plan the number of guides.
Refreshments will be served in the garden if the weather allows it.
Last year more than three hundred people attended the open day, and many of them joined as members afterwards.
The programme for this year will be published on the events page a month before the date.

Our services are designed to support small organisations that do not have the resources to manage large projects alone.
We offer advice on planning, help with applications and practical support during the delivery of a project.
The advice service is available by appointment on weekdays, either at the office or by video call.
Most appointments last about an hour and are free of charge for members.
For more complex questions, we can arrange a series of meetings with a specialist from our network.
The network includes experienced practitioners who have worked on similar projects in other regions.
We keep a record of every question we receive, and the most common ones are answered on the help page.

The help page answers the questions we receive most often about membership, events and the programme.
If you cannot find the answer to your question, you can send it to the office through the contact form.
We try to reply to every message within three working days.
Please do not send personal documents through the contact form, as it is not designed for confidential information.
For questions about an existing application, include the reference number that was sent to you by email.

Membership is open to individuals and organisations that share the aims of the programme.
Members receive the monthly newsletter, invitations to events and early access to new publications.
Organisations that join as members can also nominate a representative for the advisory group.
The advisory group meets four times a year to review the direction of the programme and to propose new priorities.
The annual membership fee helps to cover the cost of events and publications.
Reduced fees are available for students, retired members and small community groups.
You can cancel your membership at any time by writing to the office.

The monthly newsletter collects the news of the past month, a list of upcoming events and a short feature on one of our projects.
Each issue also includes a section where members can share their own news and announcements.
If you would like to contribute to the newsletter, send us a short text and a photograph before the tenth of the month.
Past issues of the newsletter are kept in the archive and can be read online.

This report describes the results of the second phase of the project and the lessons we learnt along the way.
The second phase focused on the northern part of the region, where fewer services were available at the start.
In the first months, the team visited each of the participating towns to understand the needs of local residents.
The visits showed that transport and access to information were the most common concerns.
Based on these findings, the project introduced a weekly information point in each town.
The information points were run by local volunteers with the support of the project team.
By the end of the phase, the information points had answered more than two thousand questions.
The report also includes recommendations for the third phase and a short list of open questions.

A detailed timeline of the project is included in the appendix to this report.
The appendix also lists the partners who contributed to each phase and the funding they provided.
Figures in the report are rounded to the nearest whole number, so some totals may not add up exactly.
Corrections to the report will be published on this page if any errors are found.

The workshop series continues this autumn with four sessions on planning, communication, evaluation and funding.
Each session combines a short presentation with practical exercises in small groups.
Participants are encouraged to bring an example from their own work to discuss during the exercises.
The sessions take place on Thursday evenings at the main office and are also streamed online.
Places are limited, so we recommend that you register early.
Materials from previous sessions are available on the resources page.

The resources page brings together guides, templates and examples that can help you plan and run a project.
All of the templates can be adapted to your own needs and used without asking for permission.
If you create a useful resource based on one of our templates, we would be happy to share it with other members.
New resources are announced in the newsletter and on the news page.

The news page lists the latest announcements from the programme, in the order they were published.
Older announcements are moved to the archive after six months.
You can subscribe to the news page to receive a message whenever a new announcement is published.

We are looking for volunteers to help with the information points, the open day and the preparation of the newsletter.
Volunteers do not need any previous experience, as we provide a short training session before they start.
Most volunteers give a few hours each month, and the schedule is agreed in advance to suit everyone.
In return, volunteers gain practical experience, meet people from across the region and receive a certificate at the end of the year.
If you are interested in volunteering, please fill in the short form on the community page.

The community page is a place for members to share news, ask questions and find partners for new projects.
Posts on the community page are reviewed by a moderator before they are published.
We ask everyone to keep the discussion friendly and to respect the privacy of other members.
Posts that contain advertising or personal information will not be published.

Our office is located in the centre of the town, a short walk from the railway station.
The office is open from Monday to Friday, from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon.
The building has step-free access, and a lift connects the ground floor to the meeting rooms upstairs.
Visitors who arrive by car can use the public car park next to the library.
During the summer months, the office closes earlier on Fridays.

The programme is funded by a combination of public grants, membership fees and donations from local businesses.
We publish our accounts every year, together with a summary of how the funds were used.
Most of the budget is spent directly on projects, while a smaller share covers the running costs of the office.
If you would like to support the programme with a donation, you can find the details on the support page.
Every donation, however small, helps us to reach more people in the region.

In the coming year, the programme will focus on three priorities: access to services, local partnerships and the training of volunteers.
Each priority will be led by a small working group that includes members, partners and staff.
The working groups will report their progress at the annual meeting in the autumn.
We will also review the structure of the programme to make sure that it remains simple and easy to join.
Your feedback is an important part of this review, and we welcome comments from every member.

The annual meeting is the most important event of the year for the members of the programme.
At the meeting, the team presents the results of the past year and the plans for the year ahead.
Members vote on the priorities for the coming year and elect the members of the advisory group.
The minutes of the annual meeting are published in the archive a few weeks after the event.

Please note that the office will be closed during the public holidays at the end of the year.
Messages sent during this period will be answered when the office opens again in January.
We would like to thank all of our members, partners and volunteers for their support throughout the year.
